THE POSSESSION CLUB / FIELD NOTES

Keep it moving.

You have four players and one job: keep the ball. There are no goals and no shooting. Your score comes from the way you play.

Find your rhythm

Move the player with the ball to open a passing lane. Your teammates find space automatically. Orange defenders press harder the longer you hold it. Pass before they arrive.

Hit the target and survive the clock to clear a court. Losing the ball too many times ends the match — how many turnovers you get depends on your difficulty tier. Earn XP, collect court stars, and travel the world.

Make something beautiful

  • Clean pass: 12 points. A return pass earns 6.
  • Triangle: A → B → C → A adds 35 points and earns 3 Energy.
  • Zone: receive in the highlighted circle for 25 extra points and 2 Energy.
  • Wall pass: bank off the boundary for 18 extra points.

Your multiplier climbs every four completed passes, up to ×5. It applies to pass, triangle, wall, and zone points and resets after a turnover. One-touch passes add 5; every tenth adds another 50.

Queue your next pass before the ball arrives. Quick releases build one-touch streaks; 10 gets the crowd going.

Focus slows the action and gives you time to find the next pass. Each round starts with an empty Energy reserve. Bonus zones earn 2 Energy, triangles earn 3 Energy, and split passes or Olé streaks earn 4 Energy. Rewards stack up to your tactic’s reserve (10 / 6 / 8 Energy); plain passes and passes slowed by Focus earn none. You cannot earn while Focus is active. After it runs out, release E or LT before using newly earned Energy.

Boost uses the same Energy reserve to make the ball carrier faster while you hold it, consuming Energy twice as fast as Focus. Use it to escape pressure, but it cannot run alongside Focus.

Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick aims and picks the teammate A will pass to — the chosen player is ringed on the court, so you can deliberately thread a lane between two defenders for SPLIT THE PRESS. X arms the wall pass, LT holds Focus, RT holds Boost, and LB shouts the selected target to the bonus zone. Start opens the pause menu. Use the D-pad and A in menus, B to go back.

Touch: move with the joystick, tap a teammate to pass, or use the Pass, Wall and Focus buttons.

Daily circuit brings a new seeded challenge each day. Endless flow gets tougher over time; each triangle adds 5 seconds. Free practice lets you learn without a turnover limit.

Progress saves on this device. The daily circuit resets at midnight UTC.
