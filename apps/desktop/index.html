THE POSSESSION CLUB / FIELD NOTES

Keep it moving.

You have four players, three possessions, and one job: keep the ball. There are no goals and no shooting. Your score comes from the way you play.

Find your rhythm

Move the player with the ball to open a passing lane. Your teammates find space automatically. Orange defenders press harder the longer you hold it. Pass before they arrive.

Hit the target and survive the clock to clear a court. Three turnovers end the match. Earn XP, collect court stars, and travel the world.

Make something beautiful

  • Clean pass: 12 points. A return pass earns 6.
  • Triangle: A → B → C → A adds 35 points and earns 1.5 seconds of Focus.
  • Zone: receive in the highlighted circle for 25 extra points and 1 second of Focus.
  • Wall pass: bank off the boundary for 18 extra points and 0.5 seconds of Focus.

Your multiplier climbs every four completed passes, up to ×5. It applies to pass, triangle, wall, and zone points and resets after a turnover. One-touch passes add 5; every tenth adds another 50.

Queue your next pass before the ball arrives. Quick releases build one-touch streaks; 10 gets the crowd going.

Focus slows the action and gives you time to find the next pass. Each round starts empty. Successful normal-speed wall passes earn 0.5 seconds, bonus zones earn 1 second, and triangles earn 1.5 seconds. Rewards stack up to your tactic’s reserve (5 / 3 / 4 seconds); plain passes and passes slowed by Focus earn none. You cannot earn while Focus is active. After it runs out, release E or LT before using newly earned Focus.

Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick aims and picks the teammate A will pass to — the chosen player is ringed on the court, so you can deliberately thread a lane between two defenders for SPLIT THE PRESS. X arms the wall pass, LT holds focus, Start opens the pause menu. Use the D-pad and A in menus, B to go back.

Touch: move with the joystick, tap a teammate to pass, or use the Pass, Wall and Focus buttons.

Daily circuit brings a new seeded challenge each day. Endless flow gets tougher over time; each triangle adds 5 seconds. Free practice lets you learn without a turnover limit.

Progress saves on this device. The daily circuit resets at midnight UTC.
